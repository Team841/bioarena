Driver Station

When enabled, the Driver Station software will prompt teams to allow Bioarena to connect rather than automatically connecting, and will preserve the ability to use the spacebar for robot disablement. Consider enabling this setting when physical E-Stop buttons are not present.

Leave blank to send nothing.
Same pixels, a different packet: Art-Net on UDP 6454 rather than E1.31 sACN on 5568. Tick it for a gateway that speaks only Art-Net.
Relays

One relay per HUB on this controller's GPIO, switching a red or a blue lamp: on while that HUB is active, off while it is not. The warning pulse before a HUB goes inactive blinks at the pulse's rhythm, and the transition-shift sweep that marks the HUB going inactive first blinks faster, so neither is lost.

Tick it for a board whose relays click on when the input is pulled low, as most opto-isolated modules do. Lamps that are lit between matches and dark while their HUB is active mean this is set the wrong way.
Hub FUEL Counter

A webcam watching the field reports the FUEL scored into both HUBs, and the arena credits each one to the shift it arrived in. Wire the counter into the field switch on the management subnet and give it a static address — not the access point, whose SSIDs carry robot traffic on team subnets the arena will not accept scores from.

Blank for no counter. Datagrams from any other address are ignored.
